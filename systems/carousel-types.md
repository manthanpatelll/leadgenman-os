# Carousel Types

The `/carousel` command picks one of these first. The type decides slide count, skeleton and the CTA style. When two fit, pick the one with the stronger cover hook.

| Type | Use when | Slides | CTA style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Listicle | A set of tools, prompts or mistakes | 8 to 10 | Comment keyword for the full list |
| Myth vs Fact | A belief my audience holds that is wrong | 7 to 8 | Save for later |
| Step by Step | A workflow I actually run | 9 to 12 | Comment keyword for the setup |
| Before and After | A measurable change from a system | 6 to 8 | Follow for the build |
| Tool Stack | What I pay for and why | 8 to 10 | Comment keyword for links |
| Story | A founder moment with one lesson | 7 to 9 | Share with a friend |
| Comparison | Two tools or two approaches | 8 | Comment which side you pick |
| Checklist | Pre-flight list for a task | 8 to 10 | Save and send |

## Skeletons

### Listicle
1. Cover: number plus promise in two lines
2. Why this list exists, one sentence
3 to 9. One item per slide: name, what it does for you, one proof line
10. CTA

### Myth vs Fact
1. Cover: the myth stated as most people say it
2. Why people believe it
3 to 6. Fact slides, each one flips a piece of the myth with proof
7. What to do instead
8. CTA

### Step by Step
1. Cover: outcome in two lines
2. What you need before step one
3 to 10. One step per slide, screenshot note on every third slide
11. The result
12. CTA

### Before and After
1. Cover: the after, stated as a number or a feeling
2. The before, honest
3 to 5. What changed, one change per slide
6. The after, with proof
7. CTA

### Tool Stack
1. Cover: total spend or total count
2. The rule I use to keep or cut a tool
3 to 8. One tool per slide: price, job, why it stays
9. What I cut this quarter
10. CTA

### Story
1. Cover: the moment, present tense
2 to 6. Beats in order, one image note per slide
7. The lesson in one sentence
8. Where I am now
9. CTA

### Comparison
1. Cover: A vs B in two lines
2. Who this is for
3 to 6. One dimension per slide, both sides on the same slide
7. My pick and why
8. CTA

### Checklist
1. Cover: the task plus "before you hit publish"
2 to 8. One check per slide, tick box drawn on the slide
9. What happens when you skip one
10. CTA

## Pattern interrupt slots

Every type gets a retention break at roughly 40 percent and 75 percent of the deck. A pattern interrupt is a slide that changes layout, not colour: a full-bleed screenshot, a single giant number, or a one-line slide with lots of space. Never two interrupts back to back.

## CTA matching

The CTA slide always matches the offer in `offers.md` for that content type. Step by Step and Tool Stack point to the community. Story and Before and After point to follow. Listicle and Checklist point to a comment keyword.
