# Hook Rubric, 20 points

Every cover hook gets scored here before a carousel is built. Ten criteria, each scored 0, 1 or 2. Maximum 20. The score is not a vibe, each number has a definition below. Score honestly, a generous score only hides a weak cover until the post flops.

## Thresholds

| Total | Action |
| - | - |
| 17 to 20 | Ship. Start the build with this hook as the slide 1 headline |
| 14 to 16 | Rewrite the two lowest criteria, rescore once. If it still sits under 17, drop it |
| 13 or below | Discard. Write a fresh set from different angles |

Ties break on criteria 1, 2 and 7 in that order. When two hooks tie again, take the shorter one.

## The ten criteria

### 1. Specific
- 0: Could be anyone's post about anything. "How I use AI for content."
- 1: One specific element, a tool or a number, but the rest is vague.
- 2: A named tool, file, command, product or number the reader can picture. "3 slash commands in Claude Code."

### 2. Curiosity gap
- 0: The hook is the whole payoff. Nothing left to swipe for.
- 1: Mild pull. The reader can guess most of the inside.
- 2: Exactly one open question the reader cannot close without swiping.

### 3. Power word
- 0: No emotional or curiosity trigger. Flat description.
- 1: A trigger is present but generic ("amazing", "game changer").
- 2: One sharp trigger doing real work: a number, "actually", "stopped", "replaced", "pay for", "inside", "the file", a named authority.

### 4. Who it is for
- 0: Nobody in particular.
- 1: Broad audience implied (creators, developers).
- 2: The reader knows within one second this is about them: a creator who codes, someone running a Skool, someone recording their Mac screen.

### 5. Payoff clarity
- 0: Unclear what the reader gets by the last slide.
- 1: The category of payoff is clear, the shape is not.
- 2: The reader knows the exact thing they walk away with: a file, a list, a system, a fix.

### 6. Fresh angle
- 0: The obvious take. Ten other creators posted this exact framing.
- 1: A known topic with one twist.
- 2: Only I can say this, or the angle contradicts what the feed expects.

### 7. Scroll readability
- 0: Over 12 words, or three lines at 72px, or needs a second read.
- 1: Fits, but one clause is doing too much.
- 2: 12 words or fewer, two clean lines, understood in one glance.

### 8. Proof signal
- 0: Pure claim. No evidence I did it.
- 1: First person, but no visible artefact.
- 2: A result, a screenshot, a shipped product or a real file backs the line, and the cover visual shows it.

### 9. Voice match
- 0: Drama cadence, fragments, "here is why", a "no X, no Y, no Z" list, or salesy.
- 1: Neutral. Correct but could be anyone.
- 2: Reads like me talking. Plain words, one idea, a little dry.

### 10. Format signal
- 0: The reader cannot tell what kind of carousel follows.
- 1: The type is implied.
- 2: The type is explicit: a number for a list, "step" or "how I" for steps, "versus" or "before" for a comparison, "the file" for a walkthrough.

## Worked example

Idea: the folder of markdown files that runs my content.

| Hook | Words |
| - | - |
| A. "My content calendar is now a folder of markdown files" | 10 |
| B. "How I plan Instagram content with AI" | 7 |

| Criterion | A | B |
| - | - | - |
| 1. Specific | 2 | 0 |
| 2. Curiosity gap | 2 | 1 |
| 3. Power word | 1 | 0 |
| 4. Who it is for | 1 | 1 |
| 5. Payoff clarity | 1 | 1 |
| 6. Fresh angle | 2 | 0 |
| 7. Scroll readability | 2 | 2 |
| 8. Proof signal | 2 | 0 |
| 9. Voice match | 2 | 1 |
| 10. Format signal | 1 | 1 |
| Total | 16 | 7 |

B is discarded. A sits at 16, so the two lowest criteria get a rewrite: power word and payoff clarity. Rewrite: "My whole content system is now 5 markdown files and 3 commands". Rescored: power word 2 (the numbers do the work), payoff clarity 2 (the reader gets the file list), format signal 2 (a numbered walkthrough). New total 19. Ships.

## How to use it

- Score all three hooks in one table, criteria as rows, hooks as columns.
- Write one sentence on why the winner won. If the sentence is about the score alone, look again at the hook itself.
- The winning hook goes on the cover word for word. Do not polish it after scoring without rescoring.
