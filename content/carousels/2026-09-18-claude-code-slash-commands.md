## Caption

I moved my whole Instagram content system into one folder and three slash commands in Claude Code, and this carousel is the first thing it produced end to end. Five markdown files: personal-info.md with how I talk, business-info.md with Lead Gen Man, PenAnywhere and TiltIt, offers.md with every offer and its price, strategy.md with the pillars and the weekly calendar, and current-data.md with the only numbers I allow in copy. If a number is not in that file, it does not get written. Then a .claude/commands folder with carousel.md, hooks.md and schedule.md.

When I type /carousel with an idea, it reads all five files plus my voice file and design system, matches the idea to one of eight carousel types, writes three cover hooks and scores each against a 20 point rubric. Under 17 gets rewritten or thrown out. Then it builds every slide with two pattern interrupts at set positions, ends with a CTA pulled from offers.md, writes the whole thing to a dated file and stops. Nothing goes to Instagram until I read it and reply approved. Then /schedule renders the slides at 4x and books the next open slot from my calendar. I still read every word. I just stopped rewriting the same context into a new chat every morning.

The lesson: the commands are not the asset. I could rewrite carousel.md in ten minutes. What took real time was writing down how I actually talk, what I actually sell and what actually performed this quarter, honestly, in plain files. That is what makes the output sound like me.
