# Content Strategy

Last reviewed 21 Sep 2026. Every idea passes through this file before I build it. If it fails here, it doesn't get made.

## The one-line formula

"I did [thing nobody thought was possible] with AI. Here's proof."

Not limited to Claude Code. Any AI tool works. The wilder and more specific the thing, the better it performs. Every winning post makes someone think "wait, you can do THAT?"

## Content pillars

1. **AI tools for creators.** Real workflows I run for my own reels, carousels, thumbnails and newsletter. Show the output, not the theory.
2. **Claude Code workflows.** Setup, skills, hooks, agents, the folder structure. Always paired with a second named tool so it's a collision, not a tip.
3. **Lead gen and personal brand growth.** How I grew to 720K+ combined across six platforms with zero paid spend. This pillar is my proof of work, not a course pitch.
4. **Building in public with PenAnywhere and TiltIt.** Shipped products, App Store listings, paid customers. Only I can say these things, which is the whole point.

## Net new value filter (mandatory)

Every idea must pass all three. Fail one, skip it.

- **Non-obvious.** Has the viewer probably never heard this? If ten other creators posted it, skip.
- **One of none.** Can only I say this? If any AI creator could post the same thing, skip.
- **Highly tactical.** Can the viewer use it today? Specific tools, specific steps, specific result.

Extra gates: can I show the live result (URL, App Store link, running agent)? Is it more than a concept explainer or a tool list? Is it more than a hot take? Is it more than news commentary? I build things and show proof. I'm not a news channel.

## Content mix (target split)

- Reels: 50%. Face, personality, screen recordings, the acquisition engine. About half of reel viewers are non-followers from day one.
- Carousels: 40%. Followers first, then explore page. The retention and saves engine. Carousels have averaged roughly five times the likes of reels in my own audit.
- Stories: 10%. Behind the scenes, polls, reposting the day's reel, replying to DMs publicly. Low production, keeps the DM channel warm.

## The funnel

1. **Reel** stops the scroll. Hook is two lines, named tools, proof on screen.
2. **Comment keyword** in the caption, wrapped in curly double quotes. This CTA gave a measured +48% lift, so it's never optional.
3. **DM** delivers the thing (setup file, prompt list, workflow). Real value in the DM itself, no bait.
4. **Newsletter** is the free next step. Weekday brief, best material given away.
5. **Skool** is the paid step for people who want the full courses, weekly live Q&A and me answering directly. Money-back guarantee removes the risk.

DM shares are weighted 3 to 5 times more than likes for non-follower reach, so the whole funnel is built around sends, not likes.

## What I never do

- No sequel callbacks. Every reel is standalone. Never "last time", never "part 2", never a defending champion.
- No loops, no fades, no blank first frame. Everything visible on frame zero, held to the last frame.
- No captions burned into the animation. Text gets added in the edit.
- No hashtags. Posts without them averaged more than double the likes of posts with them.
- No short captions. 500+ word captions outperformed medium ones by a wide margin.
- Only the six live platforms get named. Deleted accounts stay deleted.
- No hot takes, no cold email content, no "future of AI" prophecy, no listicles without a collision, no me-focused "I automated my whole life" stories.
- No reposts or watermarked clips. Original content gets 40 to 60% more distribution and heavy reposting gets an account excluded from recommendations.
- Never reproduce another creator's script, artwork or curated list. Formats are free, content isn't.
