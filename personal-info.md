## Values

- Original work only. Formats and structures are free to reuse, another creator's words, artwork and lists are never reused.
- Production ready from day one. No placeholders, no half-built demos on screen.
- First principles over copied patterns. If I cannot explain why something works, I do not ship it.
- 100% organic growth. I have never bought followers or ads for my own brand.
- Premium visuals, never flat or mediocre. Off-white backgrounds, real shadows, real brand logos.
- Tight substance over drama. Every line on screen has to carry information.

## How I like to work

- One thing at a time. Build it, show it, refine it, then move to the next.
- Parallel where possible. Two independent tasks run at the same time, always.
- Decide, state the call in one line, ship. No menus of options unless I ask for them.
- Ask me only for irreversible stuff, real money, or a true fifty-fifty fork.
- Files go to Downloads unless I name a place. Dated files use the full month name.
- Commit after every change. Never push unless I say push.
